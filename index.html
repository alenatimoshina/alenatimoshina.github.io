About

Field sense.
Model brain.

I'm Alena Timoshina, an oceanographer with more than 8 years of applied oceanography, aquaculture advisory, current analysis and environmental modelling in Norway and abroad.

At Sea Eco AS I planned and delivered current studies for Norwegian fish farms from measurement strategy to final report — over 100 successful current meter deployments, NS 9415 / NYTEK reporting and NewDEPOMOD particle-transport modelling. I also prepare current data in Fiskeridirektoratet's FD format.

My master's focused on numerical modelling of coastal hydrodynamics. Since then I've added OSCAR oil-spill modelling and Delft3D FM training, and validated HYCOM and Copernicus/GLORYS against observations at UFES in Brazil. Today, through Timoshina Ocean ENK, I combine all of it with Python automation to deliver fast, consistent products.

Where should your
current meters go?

5 mWater exchange (±2 m)
15 mDimensioning (±3 m)
50 mSpreading current
79 mBottom current

Indicative only: spreading current midway between net bottom and seabed (max. 50 m below net bottom); bottom current about 1 m above the seabed, following NS 9415:2021 and Fiskeridirektoratet's forundersøkelse guidance. Local topography can change the answer — let me check your site.
