About Me

Ocean Science
Meets Applied Solutions

I am a physical oceanographer with a Master's in Applied Hydrometeorology from the Russian State Hydrometeorological University, and over a decade of international fieldwork and consulting experience.

My expertise spans hydrodynamic modeling, ocean current monitoring, marine oil spill simulations, and aquaculture site analysis — with hands-on fieldwork from the Arctic Ocean to the coral reefs of Brazil.

As the founder of Timoshina Ocean (ENK), I deliver specialized oceanographic reports, model simulations, and environmental analyses for aquaculture companies and coastal planners across Scandinavia and Latin America.
